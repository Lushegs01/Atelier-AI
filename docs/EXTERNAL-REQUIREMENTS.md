Operational limitations: Qwen editing produces one still image per request with a roughly1K target. It is experimental and does not provide specialized fitting guarantees or video. The optional FASHN adapter retains its documented balanced1K photo and5/10sec video variants. Existing jobs remain pinned to their original backend. Compute costs remain a business cost even with open model weights. Account deletion and data exports require staffed operator review. No GPU inference, GPU capacity benchmark, production load test, mobile Safari installation, hosted independent-connection test or live external acceptance has been completed. The initial report is historical; see QWEN-VERIFICATION.md for subsequent evidence.
